## Merge policy

High- and medium-confidence changes were applied; low-confidence ones stay in the candidate files. When several passes proposed the same word, their evidence was merged into one entry. Every entry now has a `canon_basis` (see [docs/methodology/lexicon.md](../../docs/methodology/lexicon.md#3-entry-fields)).

## Open items

1. Localized text mining beyond the client: the shipped tables hold only 14 elven lines, and only 2 of their 42 renderings translate. The older Kael'thas lines whose Chinese glosses the wiki reports are server-side, so those glosses are still unverified.
2. Skyborne voice lines: the 196 extracted files are machine-transcribed ([report 5](5-voice-transcription.md)). A person has listened to the 12 elvish files: *banu* is stressed ba-NU every time, and the final *h* of *alah* is usually heard, but not clearly. More listening would settle their vowels and the middle word of *aradash ... banu*. The other 392 files (236 combat sounds, 106 error lines, 50 emote sounds) are transcribed too and hold no elvish word.
3. Zephras Isle quest text is served by Blizzard's servers and is cached only after a Skyborne character plays the zone.
4. Ancient elvish: the shared core is built ([report 7](7-ancient-elvish.md)) but not yet in the lexicon. Seven core parts have no gloss of their own: *aran*, *thoras*, *ethil*, *nar*, *nal*, *anu* and *ash*.
5. Reconstruction audit ([report 8](8-reconstruction-audit.md)): 28 low-confidence proposals are held back, among them *mal* "your", whose meaning still rests on elimination within one phrase; the grammar (section 5.2, canon, weak) and both translators use it anyway. The 59 older generated words record no method or seed. A native speaker has not reviewed the Ukrainian glosses of the new words.
6. Warcraft III ([report 9](9-warcraft-iii.md), [report 11](11-forsaken-kingdom-voice.md)): the 20 Forsaken Kingdom maps are encrypted; the campaign's 1,233 voice files are machine-transcribed and hold no elvish word apart from the names *Quel'Thalas* and *Ilastar*, but no person has listened, and the machines confuse *f* and *th* at four points, two of them in the Orc04 Sentinel lines. 11-candidates.json (2 fixes, 3 low-confidence adds) is not applied yet. The zhTW and zhCN unit subtitles, which may translate some of the 21 unglossed unit lines, are not downloaded. 9-candidates.json (2 adds, 4 fixes, 28 notes) is not applied yet.
7. Midnight ([report 10](10-midnight-wiki.md)): the wiki leaves out many on-click and gossip lines, and its spoken and quest lines are not independently confirmed outside the wiki. 10-candidates.json (12 fixes, 3 adds, 2 notes) is not applied yet.

Done since: the translator now matches whole canon phrases first, follows the November 2026 grammar (compounds only for names, head-first "of" phrases, *anar'* only for a "By" that opens a clause, the pronouns of section 5, no plurals) and reads apostrophe words whole in the reverse direction (bugs 1 to 4, 7, 12 and 13 in report 1, and the code half of bug 8, fixed on 2026-11-18; scores in [report 1, Method 8](1-review-and-methods.md#method-8-translator-evaluation-on-canon-phrases)).
