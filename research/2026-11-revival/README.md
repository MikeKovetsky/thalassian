The raw client extract is not in git: it is Blizzard data and stays in the local, gitignored `.wowf-extract/` folder. `tools/wowf/run_all.sh` rebuilds it. The localized dialogue tables behind report 4 sit in `.wowf-extract/localized/`, downloaded from [wago.tools](https://wago.tools/db2/BroadcastText).

## Merge policy

High- and medium-confidence changes were applied; low-confidence ones stay in the candidate files. When several passes proposed the same word, their evidence was merged into one entry. Every entry now has a `canon_basis` (see [docs/methodology/lexicon.md](../../docs/methodology/lexicon.md#3-entry-fields)).

## Open items

1. Localized text mining beyond the client: the shipped tables hold only 14 elven lines, and only 2 of their 42 renderings translate. The older Kael'thas lines whose Chinese glosses the wiki reports are server-side, so those glosses are still unverified.
2. Skyborne voice lines: the 196 extracted files are machine-transcribed ([report 5](5-voice-transcription.md)). A person has listened to the 12 elvish files: *banu* is stressed ba-NU every time, and the final *h* of *alah* is usually heard, but not clearly. More listening would settle their vowels and the middle word of *aradash ... banu*. The 392 combat files listed in the client are not extracted yet.
3. Zephras Isle quest text is served by Blizzard's servers and is cached only after a Skyborne character plays the zone.

Done since: the translator now matches whole canon phrases first, builds modifier-first compounds and reads apostrophe words whole in the reverse direction (bugs 1 to 4 and 12 in report 1, fixed on 2026-11-18; scores in [report 1, Method 8](1-review-and-methods.md#method-8-translator-evaluation-on-canon-phrases)).
