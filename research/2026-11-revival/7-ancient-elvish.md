# Ancient elvish: the shared core

Date: 2026-11-18. This uses the sources that [report 6](6-elven-origins.md) ranked: Nazja, Shalassian at full size, names and speech from before the Sundering, and Titan as a donor to check. Zandali is a control. All forms come from warcraft.wiki.gg, each with its page and anchor, and from the lexicon's own Thalassian, Darnassian, Shalassian and Skyborne entries. No paid APIs and no downloads; the lexicon is unchanged.

## Findings

1. **Elven branches share word parts far more often than chance allows.** Of each daughter branch's parts, 7–17% recur in two other branches or more. For Zandali the figure is 2%, and for Titan it is 0%.
2. **16 parts are shared by two daughter branches or more and are also found before the Sundering.** These are the strongest core: *dorei*, *quel*, *thalas*, *adore*, *zin*, *shen*, *alah*, *belore*, *aran*, *thoras*, *ethil*, *thero*, *tel*, *dor*, *nar* and *nal*.
3. ***dorei* "children, people" is in all six witnesses.** They are the five daughter branches and the names from before the Sundering: *kaldorei*, *sin'dorei*, *shal'dorei*, *shen'dorei*, Nazja *Ba'anthalso-dorei*, and the ancient *Quel'dorei*.
4. **Two Darnassian phrases are heard from Highborne of Azshara's era.** Highborne out of the Thaldraszus time rifts say *Tor ilisar'thera'nal!* ("Let our enemies beware!") and *Fandu-dath-belore?* ("Who goes there?"), each next to its English. Blizzard's gloss already calls the first a war cry used since ancient times. The rifts tie into an alternate timeline, so this is a strong hint, not proof.
5. **Nazja adds little.** It has no translated phrases, and only *dorei*, *adore*, *thero*, *zin*, *ash* and *ana* link it to the other branches.
6. **The Titan loan cannot be shown.** Lore says the early night elves took Titan words, but no Titan part recurs in the elven branches. *kal-* "star" in *kaldorei* would fit *Kalimdor* "land of eternal starlight", but *kal* is also a Zandali part, so it is at chance level.
7. **Meanings shift between branches.** *alah* is "light" in Thalassian *Anar'alah* but "to you" in Darnassian *Ishnu-alah*. *shal* is "night" in *shal'dorei* but part of "aunt" in *shal'na* and *shal'nar*. A shared part does not mean a shared meaning.

## The core

Tier 1 is a part found before the Sundering and in two daughter branches or more. Tier 2 is a part found in three daughter branches or more, but not before the Sundering. "Meaning" gives only what Blizzard glosses, for the part or a compound that contains it, as the lexicon records it.

Left out of the core, with the reason:
- *kal*, *shan* and *tor* are tier 1, but Zandali has them too. Three-letter parts can match by chance.
- *azsh*, *azshari* and *ari* are tier 1, but they all come from one name, Azshara: *Zin-Azshari*, and the naga tribe *Azsh'ari*.
- 161 tier-3 parts are found before the Sundering and in one daughter branch or none. Most are personal names. They are in [7-core.json](7-core.json).

## Method

**Sources** ([7-forms.json](7-forms.json), 420 forms):
- **Nazja:** 34 forms (phrases, words, the Nazja page's proper nouns and the naga tribes).
- **Shalassian:** 51 forms (the primer, 27 untranslated phrases, nightborne quotes and place names), on top of the lexicon's own entries.
- **Before the Sundering:** 131 forms: 113 names of places, people, groups and artifacts, each with the wiki's evidence that it dates from that era, and 18 lines spoken by Highborne and kaldorei of that era.
- **Titan:** 36 forms, used only as a control.
- **Zandali:** 168 forms from the Zandali page, the other control.

Left out of the sources:
- 22 forms found only in the old tabletop RPG, which is no longer canon.
- 26 places the naga hold. Many are ancient elven ruins, so they say nothing about Nazja.
- The retail Shalassian scrambler list (142 words) and the Titan one, which carry sound, not meaning. They are Blizzard data, so they stay in the local, gitignored `.wowf-extract/origins/`. 40 of the Shalassian list's words are real quest words, such as *Kanesh* and *Tenu'balah*.

Server-side speech for this era (report 6, source 4) came from the wiki's quote sections. The wago.tools BroadcastText export holds only the rows shipped in the game client (report 4), so a download would not have added these lines.

**Matching** ([scripts/shared_core.py](scripts/shared_core.py)):
1. Each form is split into parts at spaces, apostrophes and hyphens. Parts shorter than three letters are dropped.
2. A part counts for a branch when one of its forms has it whole. It also counts when a solid compound is it plus another known part, as *kaldorei* is *kal* + *dorei*.
3. A phrase found word for word in several branches counts once, for the first branch in the order Darnassian, Thalassian, Shalassian, Skyborne, Nazja. Thalyssra's *Tor ilisar'thera'nal* is Darnassian.
4. Titan and Zandali never count as branches. Instead, the script reports how many of their parts would qualify, which measures how often unrelated words match by chance.

To reproduce, from the repo root:

```bash
python3 research/2026-11-revival/scripts/shared_core.py research/2026-11-revival/7-forms.json --out research/2026-11-revival/7-core.json
```

It prints tier 1: 22, tier 2: 5, tier 3: 161 (before the three exclusions above), and the control rates: Titan 0 of 44, Zandali 4 of 194.

## Limits

1. **Sound laws are still out of reach.** Report 1 found no regular sound correspondence, so the core lists shared parts, not reconstructed proto-forms.
2. **A shared part may be a borrowing, not an inheritance.** The branches met again: high elves and night elves after the Third War, and the nightborne and the other elves in *Legion*. Only the parts found before the Sundering are dated.
3. **Spellings vary on the wiki itself**: *Tenu'balah* and *Tanu'balah*, *Ash'thoras denil* and *danil*, *dalees-na* and *dales-na*. Each spelling was kept as its own form.
4. **Glosses come from the wiki and the sources it cites.** Nothing was checked in the game.
