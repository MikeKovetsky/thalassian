# Ancient elvish: the shared core

Date: 2026-11-18. This uses the sources that [report 6](6-elven-origins.md) ranked: Nazja, Shalassian at full size, names and speech from before the Sundering, and Titan as a donor to check. Zandali is a control. All forms come from warcraft.wiki.gg, each with a link to its wiki page or section, and from the lexicon's own Thalassian, Darnassian, Shalassian and Skyborne entries. No paid APIs. Besides the wiki pages, only the retail Shalassian scrambler list was fetched, from wago.tools as report 6 planned, and it stays local. The lexicon is unchanged.

## Findings

1. **Elven branches share word parts far more often than chance allows.** Of each daughter branch's parts, 7–17% recur in two other branches or more. For Zandali the figure is 2%, and for Titan it is 0%.
2. **16 parts are shared by two daughter branches or more and are also found before the Sundering:** *dorei*, *quel*, *thalas*, *adore*, *zin*, *shen*, *alah*, *belore*, *aran*, *thoras*, *ethil*, *thero*, *tel*, *dor*, *nar* and *nal*. Half of them lean on a single old name or line (Limits 2). The other eight are the strongest core: *dorei*, *quel*, *adore*, *alah*, *aran*, *ethil*, *tel* and *nar*.
3. ***dorei* "children, people" is in all six witnesses.** They are the five daughter branches and the names from before the Sundering: *kaldorei*, *sin'dorei*, *shal'dorei*, *shen'dorei*, Nazja *Ba'anthalso-dorei*, and the ancient *Quel'dorei*.
4. **Elves of Azshara's era speak seven Darnassian phrases.** Highborne out of the Thaldraszus time rifts say *Tor ilisar'thera'nal!* ("Let our enemies beware!") and *Fandu-dath-belore?* ("Who goes there?"), each next to its English, and Blizzard's gloss already calls the first a war cry used since ancient times. Highborne banshees in Nazjatar say *Shaha... lor'ma...* ("Thank you"). Ghosts of night elves from that era say *Bandu thoribas!*, *Ishnu-alah*, *Elune-adore* and *Ande'thoras-ethil*. The rifts tie into an alternate timeline, and the ghosts and banshees speak in *Legion* and *Battle for Azeroth*, so this is a strong hint, not proof.
5. **Nazja adds little.** It has no translated phrases, and of the core only *dorei*, *adore*, *thero*, *zin*, *ash* and *ana* are found in it. Its 7% is the one daughter rate that is not clearly above Zandali's.
6. **The Titan loan cannot be shown.** Lore says the early night elves took Titan words, but no Titan part is found in two elven branches. Three turn up elsewhere: *Kalimdor* itself, a name the ancient elves used; *val* (*Val'anyr*), as in the ancient *Val'sharah*; and *amir*, as in Darnassian *amir* "harmony". *kal-* "star" in *kaldorei* would fit *Kalimdor* "land of eternal starlight", but *kal* is also a Zandali part, so it is at chance level.
7. **Meanings shift between branches.** *alah* is "light" in Thalassian *Anar'alah* but "to you" in Darnassian *Ishnu-alah*. *shal* is "night" in *shal'dorei* but part of "aunt" in *shal'na* and *shal'nar*. A shared part does not mean a shared meaning.

## The core

Tier 1 is a part found before the Sundering and in two daughter branches or more. Tier 2 is a part found in three daughter branches or more, but not before the Sundering. Every part here is inferred, not canon: no Blizzard source calls any of them ancient elvish. "Meaning" gives only what Blizzard glosses, for the part or a compound that contains it, as the lexicon records it.

Left out of the core, with the reason:
- *kal* and *tor* are tier 1, but Zandali has them too. Three-letter parts can match by chance.
- *azsh*, *azshari* and *ari* are tier 1, but they all come from one name, Azshara: *Zin-Azshari*, and the naga tribe *Azsh'ari*.
- 157 tier-3 parts are found before the Sundering and in one daughter branch or none. Most are personal names, and some are English-looking surnames such as *Stormrage* (see Sources). They are in [7-core.json](7-core.json).

## Method

**Sources** ([7-forms.json](7-forms.json), 419 forms):
- **Nazja:** 34 forms (phrases, words, the Nazja page's proper nouns and the naga tribes).
- **Shalassian:** 50 forms (the primer, the 27 untranslated phrases and the page's other words, nightborne quotes and place names), on top of the lexicon's own entries. *Shan'do* is not one of them: the wiki says the nightborne word for "honored teacher" is unknown.
- **Before the Sundering:** 131 forms: 113 names of places, people, groups and artifacts (two of them titles, *shan'do* and *thero'shan*), each with the wiki's evidence that it dates from that era, and 18 lines spoken by Highborne and kaldorei of that era. English words around a name or line (*Lord*, *Crystal of*, *Our people...*) are left out of the form, and the wiki's full text stays in the record's `name` or `line`. English-looking surnames such as *Stormrage* stay, because Blizzard glosses one of them, *Sunstrider* "He who walks the day", as Darnassian.
- **Titan:** 36 forms, used only as a control.
- **Zandali:** 168 forms from the Zandali page, the other control.

Left out of the sources:
- 22 forms found only in the old tabletop RPG, which is no longer canon.
- 26 places the naga hold. Many are ancient elven ruins, so they say nothing about Nazja.
- The retail Shalassian scrambler list (141 words) and the client's Titan list, which carry sound, not meaning. They are Blizzard data, so they stay in the local, gitignored `.wowf-extract/origins/`. The 39 Shalassian words that the Darnassian list lacks (report 2) are nearly all real quest words, such as *Kanesh* and *Tenu'balah*.
- The Chinese and Korean versions of the server-side lines below (the localization half of report 6, source 4). They are not in the wago.tools export either.

Server-side speech for this era (report 6, source 4) came from the wiki's quote sections. The wago.tools BroadcastText export holds only the rows shipped in the game client (report 4), so a download would not have added these lines.

**Matching** ([scripts/shared_core.py](scripts/shared_core.py)):
1. Each form is split into parts at spaces, apostrophes and hyphens. Parts shorter than three letters are dropped.
2. A part counts for a branch when one of its forms has it whole. It also counts when a solid compound is it plus another known part, as *kaldorei* is *kal* + *dorei*.
3. A phrase found word for word in several branches counts once, for the first branch in the order Darnassian, Thalassian, Shalassian, Skyborne, Nazja. Thalyssra's *Tor ilisar'thera'nal* is Darnassian. A single word, such as *alah*, counts in every branch that has it.
4. Titan and Zandali never count as branches. Instead, the script reports how many of their parts would qualify, which measures how often unrelated words match by chance.

To reproduce, from the repo root:

```bash
python3 research/2026-11-revival/scripts/shared_core.py research/2026-11-revival/7-forms.json --out research/2026-11-revival/7-core.json
```

It prints tier 1: 21, tier 2: 5, tier 3: 157 (before the exclusions above), and the control rates: Titan 0 of 44, Zandali 3 of 194.

## Limits

1. **Sound laws are still out of reach.** Report 1 found no regular sound correspondence, so the core lists shared parts, not reconstructed proto-forms.
2. **Half of tier 1 leans on a single old name or line.** The quotation rule works only among the daughters. So a daughter that has a part only in the very name, title or line that dates it still counts as a branch. That is how eight parts reach tier 1:
   - *thalas*: Darnassian *Eldre'Thalas*, and the *Nar'thalas* in Shalassian *Nar'thalas vas manari*, are the ancient cities themselves.
   - *shen*: Darnassian *Shen'dralar* is the ancient order, and *Shen'dralas* is a new zone that shares its name.
   - *zin*: Darnassian *zin* is cut from *Zin-Azshari*, the ancient capital.
   - *thero*: Darnassian *thero* is cut from *Thero'shan*, the title that dates it.
   - *dor*: Shalassian *Arcan'dor* is the ancient kind of tree.
   - *belore*, *thoras* and *nal*: Darnassian *Fandu-dath-belore*, *Ande'thoras-ethil* and *Tor ilisar'thera'nal* are the lines the ghosts and time-rift Highborne say.

   Counted once, these eight would be tier 3. The other eight have at least two daughters each that show the part in words of their own.
3. **Six tier-1 parts are dated only by speech**, not by a name: *alah*, *adore*, *ethil*, *belore*, *thoras* and *nal*. The speakers are ghosts in *Legion* and time-rift Highborne in *Dragonflight*. Report 6 asked for a part in three branches or in a name from before the Sundering. *alah* and *adore* are in three branches, but *ethil*, *belore*, *thoras* and *nal* pass only if such speech counts like a name.
4. **A shared part may be a borrowing, not an inheritance.** The branches met again: high elves and night elves after the Third War, and the nightborne and the other elves in *Legion*. Only the parts found before the Sundering are dated.
5. **Spellings vary on the wiki itself**: *Tenu'balah* and *Tanu'balah*, *Ash'thoras denil* and *danil*, *dalees-na* and *dales-na*. Each spelling was kept as its own form.
6. **Glosses come from the wiki and the sources it cites.** Nothing was checked in the game.
